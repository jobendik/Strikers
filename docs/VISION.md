# Vision

**Strikers '26 should make someone open a browser tab and ask "how is this running
in a browser?" — and still be playing thirty minutes later because the football is
good.**

The benchmark is the *experience* of a modern AAA football game (responsiveness,
depth, presentation, atmosphere) delivered as an original game: no proprietary
assets, likenesses, branding or code.

## Pillars (in priority order)

1. **Every touch means something.** The ball is a physical object the player
   influences, never a token attached to an avatar. Goals feel earned.
2. **Easy to learn, hard to master.** Four buttons play a full match; the stick,
   timing and modifiers add expression. Assists tidy results — they never play for
   you. Difficulty is intelligence, not cheating.
3. **Players are different.** Pace, acceleration, agility, strength, touch and
   composure are visible in how each player moves and handles the ball.
4. **Teams think.** Shape, support, cover, pressing, transitions — recognisable
   football, with tactical identities.
5. **A stadium that is alive.** Broadcast cameras, crowd that reacts to the match
   it is watching, sound with anticipation and release.
6. **Matches tell stories — honestly.** Late winners and comebacks emerge from the
   simulation; nothing is scripted, rubber-banded or nudged.
7. **Runs everywhere, shows off on good hardware.** Scalable tiers from a mid
   phone to a desktop GPU; measured, not claimed.
8. **Ethical by construction.** No real money, no pay-to-win, no dark patterns,
   simulated services labelled as simulated. Saves are never broken.

## What we will not do

- Trade enjoyable play for realism for its own sake.
- Ship placeholder modes to inflate a feature list.
- Claim performance or quality without evidence, or call AI observation "playtesting".

See `MASTER_PLAN.md` for milestones, `QUALITY_STANDARD.md` for the bar and the
current honest scores.
